Ultron

Ultron is a private personal assistant that runs on its owner's own computer.

It answers questions, keeps notes, and helps its owner with mail, calendar and documents. The assistant and its memory live on that one computer. This is a personal project with a single user. It is not a public service and there is nothing to sign up for.

Google sign-in

When its owner signs in with Google, Ultron can read and draft mail, read and add calendar events, and read files in Drive, only for that owner and only when asked. The privacy note explains what happens to that information.
